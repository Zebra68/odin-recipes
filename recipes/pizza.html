Pizza Dough

This bread machine pizza dough makes two 13-inch pizzas with a crisp yet chewy crust. The dough is ready to use as soon as the DOUGH cycle ends—no overnight rest or refrigeration required.

    Ingredients
  • 1 cup + 1 tablespoon (241 g) cool water
  • 2 tablespoons (28 g) olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon (21 g) honey
  • 3 cups (360 g) bread flour
  • 1 teaspoon table salt
  • 1 teaspoon granulated sugar
  • 2 teaspoons instant or bread machine yeast
    Steps:
  1. Mix all the ingridients in the bread machine pan. It is generally good practice to add all liquids first, then add solids. Select the DOUGH cycle, then START.
  2. Check after 15 minutes to see the dough consistency. The dough should stick to the sides, then pull away cleanly. - If it sticks without releasing, add 1 teaspoon of flour every minute until it holds its shape. - If its too dry, add 1 teaspoon of water every minute until it sticks briefly to one side before releasing.
  3. At the end of the bread machine cycle, preheat the oven to 500˚F (260˚C).
  4. Turn the dough onto your work surface. Pour a small amount of olive oil on your hands then gently kneed the dough to prevent it from sticking to the work surface. Divide into two balls, cover, and let rest for 10 minutes.
  5. Stretch the dough to fit the pizza pans. If it resists, let it rest before trying again.
  6. Spread a thin layer of sauce, followed by cheese and toppings.
  7. Lower to oven temp to 450°F (230°C) when the pizza goes in. Bake for 10-15 minutes until the crust is golden and crisp.

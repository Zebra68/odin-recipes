Spicy BLT Wrap

A twist on the classic BLT. Lighter on carbs as a wrap and sriracha adds a mild spicy flavor.

Ingredients (per serving):

  • 2 slices of bacon
  • 1 tablespoon of mayonnaise
  • 1/4 teaspoon sriracha sauce
  • 3 romaine lettuce leaves
  • 6 cherry tomatoes, cut in half
  • 10-inch flour tortilla

Steps:

  1. Cook bacon until lightly crisp.
  2. Mix mayonaise and sriracha together in a small bowl.
  3. Trim ends of lettuce and then chop into 1-inch ribbons.
  4. Add lettuce and tomatoes to the bowl with the mavonnaise mixture. Stir until well coated.
  5. Spoon the lettuce and tomato mixture onto the tortilla and spread, leaving a 2-inch ring around the edges.
  6. Break the bacon pieces in half and place in the center of the tortilla. Fold up the bottom of the tortilla over the filling, then roll to create a wrap.
